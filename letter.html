To my Bauni, Aditi ❤️

Happy Birthday, Aditi! 🥹🎂❤️

I don't even know how to start this letter because there are so many things I want to tell you, but I'll start with the story of how we happened.

It's actually crazy when I think about it.

On 30 October, we came into a relationship, but the story had already started before that. I remember having a crush on you, but I had no idea that you liked me too.

Then your friend texted me and told me that you had a crush on me. 😭
The funniest part? I didn't even see the message at that time. 💀

And then the very next day, I went to school and asked you for your Instagram ID.

And you casually said that you already had mine.

BRO. 😭😭

I was genuinely shocked. Like, "Wait… how do you already have my Instagram?" 😂

Looking back now, that little moment feels so special because I didn't know it then, but somehow you already knew about me, and I was slowly finding out that you liked me too.

And somehow, from a crush, a few messages, a little bit of stalking 😭, and all those small moments, we ended up becoming us on 30 October.

I don't think either of us could've guessed that something that started so randomly would become something this special.

And that's what I love about our story. It wasn't some perfect movie scene. It was random, unexpected, slightly chaotic, and completely us. 🥹❤️

On your birthday, I just want you to know how grateful I am that you came into my life.

You're not just someone I love—you've become someone whose presence makes my ordinary days feel a little more special.

So today, I hope you smile a lot, laugh a lot, get everything you wish for, and have the kind of birthday you'll remember for a long time.

Happy Birthday, my Bauni. ❤️

Thank you for being part of my life and for turning one random crush into one of my favorite stories.

And honestly…

Who would've thought that asking for an Instagram ID would lead to all this? 😭❤️

Happy Birthday, Aditi.
I’m really glad it was you. 🫶
